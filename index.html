AI Data Handling Quiz

Knowing what you can share, and where

Before you begin

AI is a tool that can help us notice, organize, draft, and learn. This quiz is not about whether to use it - it is about what information you put into it. Before you put company information into an AI tool, choose a color.

Use AI to make the work better. Never use it to hand away responsibility.

Three kinds of AI tool

Company-approved

Contracted, with a data processing agreement, a no-training clause, MFA, and audit logging. Approved for company work.

Department-approved

Approved for one specific use with a limited data scope. Check before you use it for something it was not approved for.

Personal or free

Consumer tools, browser extensions, personal accounts. Never for company information - general learning only.

Watch what you add along the way. An AI output inherits the highest classification of any input that produced it - so a Green task turns Red the moment you paste Red information into it.
